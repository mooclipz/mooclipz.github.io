Mo Manager – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Mo Manager is a real-time video clip creation platform that allows users to create, edit and share clips with friends. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Mo Manager, we may process:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, such as your display name and profile picture, when authorized.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Video clips created or selected by users.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate Mo Manager.
  • Connect users' TikTok accounts.
  • Enable users to upload their clips to TikTok.
  • Maintain and improve the service.
  • Protect the security of our application.

4. TikTok Integration

Mo Manager uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API.

With user authorization, we use the user.info.basic scope to access basic profile information and the video.upload scope to upload videos initiated by users.

Data obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the requested integration features and is handled in accordance with applicable TikTok developer requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with TikTok when necessary to provide features explicitly requested by users, such as uploading videos. We may also use service providers necessary to operate Mo Manager.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service or meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their personal information and connected account data by contacting us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. You can contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Mo Manager is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law or TikTok's policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
